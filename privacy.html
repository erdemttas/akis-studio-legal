Akış Studio Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 16, 2026

Akış Studio is designed to run locally on your computer. Video files, project data, and connected account details are kept in the local application data folder unless you choose to send content to a connected service.

Information used

The application may use video files, captions, publishing details, and authorization tokens needed to perform an action you request.

Third-party services

If enabled by you, audio may be sent to Groq for transcription, and finished videos may be sent to Instagram, YouTube, or TikTok for publishing. Each service processes data under its own privacy policy.

Storage and control

Account connections are controlled from the application. You can disconnect an account and remove local project files from your computer.

Contact

For questions about this policy, contact the person who provided or operates this Akış Studio installation.
